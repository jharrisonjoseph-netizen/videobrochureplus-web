Real Estate Video Brochures for Property Marketing

Present a property, development or real estate service with preloaded video and printed details in one physical piece that can be replayed without relying on the recipient to open a link.

Plan a property presentationReview sample options

Quick answer: a real estate video brochure is most useful when the property opportunity is valuable, the recipient list is selective and visual context matters. It can support a listing pitch, development launch, investor presentation or targeted buyer follow-up.

Where the format fits

Listing presentations

Introduce the agency, marketing plan, previous work and seller experience in a leave-behind that supports the in-person pitch.

Property developments

Combine renderings, walkthroughs, location footage, amenities and printed specifications for buyers, brokers or investors.

Luxury property outreach

Send a protected presentation to a selected buyer or intermediary when the property story warrants a higher-touch format.

Commercial real estate

Explain site access, tenant mix, fit-out, logistics, floor plans or investment context with motion and printed reference details.

Investor communication

Present a development vision, project team and key milestones while keeping legal and financial information in approved documents.

Open-house follow-up

Give a qualified prospect a replayable property overview and a persistent contact path after a viewing or private event.

Build a clear property story

Start with the audience. A seller presentation needs a different opening from a development launch or investor briefing.
Show the place. Use walkthroughs, aerial footage, renderings, neighborhood context or facilities where they improve understanding.
Keep details legible. Avoid dense plans or small captions on compact screens; place durable specifications in print or a linked document.
Use approved claims. Confirm availability, dimensions, completion dates, pricing statements and regulated disclosures before video loading and printing.
Give one next step. Print the responsible contact, phone, URL or QR code and state the intended action in the final video frame.

Real estate video brochure FAQ

What can a real estate video brochure show?

It can combine a property walkthrough, development overview, location context, amenities, agent or developer introduction and printed contact details in one physical presentation.

Which screen size works for property marketing?

Five- and seven-inch screens are common planning directions for portable presentations, while 10.1-inch screens provide more viewing area for premium development presentations. Final size depends on content, pack dimensions and delivery method.

Can each property brochure use different video?

Project-specific video loading can be planned, but personalization workflow, file naming, quantity and verification requirements should be confirmed before production.

Should we approve a sample?

Yes. A physical sample lets the property team review image quality, printed details, playback, controls, charging, construction and protective packaging before the full run.
