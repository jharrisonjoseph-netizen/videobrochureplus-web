Video Business Cards

A video business card places a compact video module inside a small printed format. It is designed for short introductions where portability matters, and requires content that remains clear on a 2.4-inch screen.

Where this format fits

Executive introductions

Leave a short company or personal introduction after a meeting.

Give selected contacts a more detailed follow-up than a printed card can hold.

Compact product stories

Show a focused product clip where a larger brochure is unnecessary.

Three decisions to make before artwork

Design for a small screen

Use large on-screen text, simple framing and a short message rather than detailed diagrams.

Protect the viewing area

Confirm the closed dimensions, screen window, fold and charging access before artwork is finalized.

Match quantity to the audience

This format is most useful for a defined recipient group rather than indiscriminate distribution.

Prepare for quotation: quantity, delivery country, target date, screen preference, finished size, video duration, artwork status and packaging requirements.

Questions buyers ask

Is this the size of a standard paper business card?

The final dimensions depend on the screen and internal electronics, so confirm the physical format before designing artwork.

What video works best?

Use a concise introduction with simple visuals and limited on-screen text that remains readable on a 2.4-inch display.

What is the starting quantity?

The current standard MOQ shown for video business cards is 100 pieces, subject to final configuration.
