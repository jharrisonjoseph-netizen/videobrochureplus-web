Video Brochures for Trade Shows & Events

Most printed handouts from a trade show are discarded before the flight home. A video brochure keeps presenting your product after the booth conversation ends.

Get pricingRequest a sample

Quick answer: Use video brochures at trade shows as a premium takeaway for qualified visitors, not as a mass giveaway. Pair them with standard literature for general traffic, and plan production around the show date with time for sample approval and shipping.

Three ways to use video brochures at an event

Qualified-visitor takeaway

Give it after a real conversation so the decision-maker can replay the demonstration and share it with colleagues.

Pre-show invitation

Mail a short video invitation to key accounts before the show to book meetings at your booth.

Plan a video mailer

Post-show follow-up

Send a personalized video to hot leads after the event, while the conversation is still fresh.

Plan the video for a busy show floor

Visitors will often watch on a plane, in a hotel or back at the office, so the video should make sense without your sales team present. Open with the problem you solve, show the product working, include one proof point such as a customer result or certification, and end with a clear next step. Use chapters if one brochure needs to serve different audiences, for example a product overview, a technical demonstration and a company introduction. Print a QR code or URL in the brochure so interested viewers can respond. The artwork and video guide covers file preparation.

Trade show production timeline

Confirm the specification early. Fix screen size, format, quantity and delivery address at least several weeks before the show.
Approve artwork and video. Final print files and the edited video are needed before sample production.
Check a physical sample. Review print color, playback, sound, buttons and charging before mass production.
Produce and test. Units are printed, assembled, loaded with video and checked before packing.
Ship with a buffer. Allow for battery shipping rules and customs, and deliver to your office or the event logistics partner, not on the opening day.
Quantity tip: order for the number of qualified conversations you expect, not total booth traffic. A smaller run of video brochures for real prospects usually delivers more value than spreading budget across every visitor. Standard video brochure orders start from 10 pieces.

Frequently asked questions

Are video brochures worth it for trade shows?

They are most effective as a takeaway for qualified visitors and key accounts, where a replayable demonstration can influence the next meeting or order. For general booth traffic, standard printed literature is usually more cost-effective.

How many video brochures should we order for an event?

Base the quantity on the number of qualified conversations or key accounts you expect, plus pre-show and post-show mailings, rather than total visitor numbers.

How early should we order video brochures for a show?

Start several weeks before the event to allow for artwork and video approval, a physical sample, production, testing and international shipping with battery documentation.

Can one brochure hold several videos?

Yes. With chapter buttons, one brochure can hold several videos, such as a product overview, technical demonstration and company introduction.

Planning for an upcoming show?

Send the event date, delivery country, quantity and screen size, and we will plan a production schedule that works backwards from the show.
