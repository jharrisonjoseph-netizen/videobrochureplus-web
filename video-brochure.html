Buyer questions

Custom video brochure FAQ

What information is needed for a quote?

Provide the screen size, finished card size, quantity, destination country, delivery deadline, video length and any preferred cover, button or packaging options.

Which screen size should I choose?

Select the screen around content detail, card dimensions, delivery method, quantity and budget. Use the screen size guide to compare common planning options.

Should we approve a physical sample?

A physical sample helps the buyer inspect print color, construction, screen playback, audio, controls, charging and packaging before mass production.

What affects the cost?

Quantity, screen, memory, battery, construction, print finish, packaging and destination are major variables. See the cost guide.

Can the video be loaded before shipment?

Video files can be prepared for loading as part of the agreed production specification. Confirm file format, number of videos, playback behavior and update requirements before approval.
