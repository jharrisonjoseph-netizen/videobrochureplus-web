Frequently asked questions

Answers that help customers move from idea to quotation faster.

What information do you need for a quotation?

Please provide screen size, card size, quantity, delivery country, expected deadline, artwork status and video file length.

Can you preload our video before shipment?

Yes. We can preload your video file before shipment. Depending on the module, the video can also be updated later by USB.

Can the brochure be printed with our logo and design?

Yes. The cover and inside pages can be fully customized with brand artwork, colors, text, QR code and product information.

Which size is best for B2B presentations?

5.0 inch and 7.0 inch are common because they balance screen impact, card size and production cost. 10.1 inch is better for premium presentations.

Do you support sample orders?

Yes. Sample orders are suitable for checking screen quality, printing effect, structure and video playback before bulk production.
