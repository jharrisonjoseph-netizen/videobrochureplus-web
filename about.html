About Video Brochure Plus

About Wuhan Superlieur Technology

Video Brochure Plus is the company's customer-facing website for custom LCD video brochures, mailers, greeting cards, folders and presentation boxes. Project and production operations are supported across Shenzhen, Wuhan and Hong Kong for international buyers.

What to include in your first message

Frequently asked questions

Which company operates this website?

Video Brochure Plus is the customer-facing brand of WUHAN SUPERLIEUR TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD, with production and project operations supported across Shenzhen, Wuhan and Hong Kong.

Do you support international projects?

Yes. Export experience includes the US, EU, Australia and UAE. Shipping method and cost depend on destination, quantity, packed dimensions, battery documentation and timeline.

How can I verify a configuration before production?

Request available samples and confirm whether a custom prototype is needed for the product, printing and packaging you plan to approve.

Prepare a clear quotation request

Send the intended use, quantity, destination, target date, screen preference and current artwork or video status.
