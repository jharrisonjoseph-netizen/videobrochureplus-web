About Video Brochure Plus

Practical Support for Custom Video-in-Print Projects

Video Brochure Plus is the customer-facing website of Wuhan Superlieur Technology Co., Ltd. The site focuses on custom LCD video brochures, mailers, greeting cards, folders and presentation boxes for international projects.

What we help buyers define

Product configuration

Translate the use case into a screen, finished format, cover direction, memory and control layout to quote.

Production files

Provide dieline guidance and review the artwork and video information needed for the selected structure.

Approval and delivery

Support physical sample review, production checks, video loading, packaging and international shipping planning.

How we describe product information

Current ranges, not blanket promises

Published sizes and MOQs are planning values for standard product directions. Written quotations confirm the selected configuration.

Evidence over unsupported claims

Product pages explain observable specifications and review points without adding unverified certifications or performance statistics.

A clear contact path

Buyers can send a structured quotation request, email the sales contact or continue the specification discussion by WhatsApp.

What to include in your first message

Project goal

Describe the audience, use case and the presentation you want recipients to experience.

Specification clues

Share the likely screen or format, quantity, video duration, packaging needs and artwork status.

Delivery details

Include the destination country and target date so production and shipping options can be evaluated together.

Frequently asked questions

Which company operates this website?

The organization identified on the website is Wuhan Superlieur Technology Co., Ltd., trading through the Video Brochure Plus customer-facing brand.

Do you support international projects?

The published project workflow includes global shipping planning. The method and cost depend on destination, quantity, packed dimensions and timeline.

How can I verify a configuration before production?

Request available samples and confirm whether a custom prototype is needed for the product, printing and packaging you plan to approve.

Prepare a clear quotation request

Send the intended use, quantity, destination, target date, screen preference and current artwork or video status.
