Format comparison

Video Brochure vs Video Mailer vs Video Box

All three put a screen and your video into a printed piece. The right choice depends on how it is delivered, what else goes inside and the impression you need.

Get pricingRequest a sample

Quick answer: Choose a video brochure for hand-delivered presentations, a video mailer when the piece must survive postal or courier delivery, and a video box when the screen must sit alongside a product sample, gift or printed inserts.

Questions that decide the format

How will it reach the recipient? Hand delivery allows a lighter build. Postal and courier delivery need protective packaging and battery shipping documents.
Does anything else go inside? A sample, gift or document set points to a video box or folder rather than a brochure.
How many recipients? Small lists of high-value accounts justify boxes. Larger campaigns usually favor brochures or mailers.
How much detail is in the video? Detailed demonstrations benefit from 7 or 10.1 inch screens. Short personal messages work on smaller screens.
What is the budget per recipient? Compare the full landed cost per piece, including packaging and shipping, not only the unit price.
Other formats: video folders hold A4/A5 documents with a 7 or 10.1 inch screen, and video business cards or video greeting cards use compact 2.4 to 5 inch screens for lighter campaigns.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a video brochure and a video mailer?

A video mailer is a video brochure packed in protective outer packaging designed for postal or courier delivery. A standard video brochure is usually hand delivered or shipped inside other packaging.

Is a video box better than a video brochure?

Not always. A video box is the better choice when the recipient should also receive a product, sample or gift, or when unboxing is part of the experience. For a presentation alone, a brochure is usually more cost-effective.

Can a video brochure be mailed?

Yes, but it needs protective packaging and shipping documents for the built-in lithium battery. That combination is what a video mailer provides.

Which format has the lowest minimum order?

Standard video brochures start from 10 pieces. Video boxes start from 50 pieces, and other formats have their own minimums.
