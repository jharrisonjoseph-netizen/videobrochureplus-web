What Determines Video Brochure Cost?

Video brochure pricing is configuration-based. Quantity and screen size are major inputs, but memory, battery, cover construction, print finishes, packaging and destination also change the final quotation.

Request a configured quoteCompare screen sizes

There is no responsible single price without a specification. A useful quote states the quantity, screen, finished dimensions, cover, memory, buttons, packaging, destination and delivery terms. Any published example should carry a date and configuration; this page therefore explains the cost model rather than inventing a universal price.

How to compare quotations

Normalize the specification

Make sure every supplier quotes the same screen, memory, battery, card dimensions, finish, packaging and quantity.

Separate included items

Confirm whether the quotation includes video loading, cables, individual packing, artwork checks, sample charges and freight.

Check the approval path

Clarify what is approved digitally, what requires a physical sample and how specification changes affect the quote.

Pricing FAQ

What affects the cost of a video brochure?

Quantity, LCD screen, memory, battery, construction, print finish, buttons, packaging, sampling and shipping are the main variables.

Can I receive a quote without final artwork?

Yes, a planning quote can be based on dimensions and finish assumptions. Final pricing should be confirmed after the specification and production files are reviewed.

Are shipping and duties included?

This depends on the quotation and trade terms. Always confirm whether freight, duties and taxes are included rather than assuming.

How can we control cost?

Start from the business requirement, select the smallest screen and simplest construction that meets it, avoid unnecessary finishes, and compare configurations at realistic quantities.
