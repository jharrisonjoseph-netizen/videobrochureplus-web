Three decisions to make before artwork

Provide product dimensions first

The insert and outer box depend on accurate item dimensions, orientation and removal method.

Balance screen and product space

A larger screen changes the lid, hinge, weight and available insert area.

Approve fit as well as appearance

The prototype review should include product fit, opening force, playback, charging access and transit protection.

Prepare for quotation: quantity, delivery country, target date, screen preference, finished size, video duration, artwork status and packaging requirements.

Questions buyers ask

Can the insert be customized?

Yes. The insert is planned around the product dimensions and presentation layout, with the final material confirmed during quotation.

What quantity can I request?

The current standard starting quantity shown for video boxes is 50 pieces. Final availability depends on the selected configuration.

What files should I prepare?

Prepare brand artwork, video files, product dimensions and any printed copy. A dieline is confirmed for the selected construction.
