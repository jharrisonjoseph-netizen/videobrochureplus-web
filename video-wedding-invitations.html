Three decisions to make before artwork

Keep essential details in print

Dates, addresses and RSVP information should remain visible without requiring video playback.

Prepare video for repeated viewing

Use clear audio, readable captions where needed and a duration appropriate for an invitation.

Test guest delivery packaging

Review the closed invitation, outer protection and address method before producing the full quantity.

Prepare for quotation: quantity, delivery country, target date, screen preference, finished size, video duration, artwork status and packaging requirements.
