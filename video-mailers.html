Questions buyers ask

What is the difference between a video brochure and a video mailer?

The brochure is the video-in-print presentation. A mailer also includes packaging planned to protect and deliver that presentation.

Can you quote shipping with the product?

Shipping depends on quantity, final packed dimensions, weight, destination and timeline, so it is confirmed after the configuration is defined.

Should the mailer be sampled?

A physical sample is useful for reviewing playback, print and construction. A packed test is also recommended when delivery protection is critical.
