Custom Video Mailers for Direct Mail

A video mailer pairs a printed LCD video card with protective packaging for delivery. It is best suited to targeted B2B campaigns where the recipient, presentation and follow-up path are defined before production.

Request a project quotePlan a sample

Choose a video mailer when the project requires both presentation and delivery protection. A standard video brochure may be used in meetings or placed inside separate packaging; a mailer project considers the outer pack, transit handling, address workflow and recipient opening experience together.

Targeted direct mail

For named prospects, account-based campaigns and follow-up packages where the content and call to action are specific.

Launch and PR kits

For product introductions where the screen explains the product before the recipient reaches the enclosed item or next step.

Executive outreach

For a limited recipient list where a physical video presentation supports a wider sales process.

Inputs required before quotation

Campaign: describe the audience, use case and desired next action.
Format: provide screen size, finished dimensions, hardcover or softcover preference and button needs.
Content: provide video length, file status, number of videos and whether files need to be updateable.
Delivery: provide quantity, destination country, deadline and any addressing or fulfillment requirements.
Approval: confirm whether you need a physical sample before mass production.

Video mailer FAQ

What is a video mailer?

It is a printed LCD video card supplied with packaging intended to protect the piece during delivery. The construction should be selected around the screen, card size, destination and fulfillment process.

Can every video brochure be mailed without another box?

No universal format is suitable for every route. Protection depends on the finished dimensions, weight, screen, battery, carrier handling and destination. Confirm the packing method before production.

What affects the project cost?

The main variables are quantity, screen, memory, battery, card construction, print finishes, outer packaging, packing requirements and shipping destination. See the cost guide.

Should we approve a sample?

A physical sample helps the buyer check print color, structure, playback, buttons, charging and the opening sequence before mass production.

Prepare a video mailer quotation

Send the quantity, screen size, finished size, destination, deadline and fulfillment requirements. Recommendations are confirmed against the final specification.
