Video brochure basics

What Is a Video Brochure?

A plain-language explanation of video brochures: what is inside, how they play, where they work best and what to decide before ordering.

Get pricingRequest a sample

Quick answer: A video brochure is a printed hardcover or softcover brochure with a thin LCD screen, speaker, rechargeable battery and memory built into the inside cover. When the recipient opens it, a preloaded video starts playing, with no internet connection, app or computer required.

How a video brochure works

Your video is loaded at the factory. Approved MP4 files are copied to the module memory and tested before shipment.
The recipient opens the cover. A magnetic or light-sensitive switch starts the video automatically.
The video plays offline. No Wi-Fi, link, QR scan or software is needed, so the message is seen exactly as approved.
Buttons add control. The viewer can pause, change volume or jump between chapters if buttons are specified.
It stays on the desk. The brochure can be replayed, recharged by USB and passed on to other decision-makers.

Video brochure formats

The same video-in-print technology comes in several formats. A standard video brochure suits most sales and marketing work. A video mailer adds protective packaging for postal or courier delivery. A video box holds a product sample or gift. Video folders carry A4 or A5 documents, while video business cards and video greeting cards use compact 2.4-inch screens. Compare them in the video brochure vs video mailer vs video box guide.

What affects the cost

Price is set by screen size, quantity, memory, cover construction, finishes, buttons, packaging and shipping destination. Larger screens and hardcover builds cost more per unit, and unit price falls as quantity rises. The video brochure cost guide explains each factor and what a complete quotation should include.

Decision rule: a video brochure is worth it when each recipient is valuable enough that a physical, memorable presentation is likely to change the next step, such as a meeting, a site visit or an order.

Frequently asked questions

What is a video brochure?

A video brochure is a printed brochure with a built-in LCD screen, speaker, rechargeable battery and memory. It plays a preloaded video automatically when opened, without internet access or software.

How does a video brochure turn on?

Most video brochures use a magnetic or light-sensitive switch that starts the video when the cover is opened and stops it when closed. Buttons can be added for play, pause, volume and chapters.

Can the video be changed later?

Many modules allow the video to be replaced by connecting the brochure to a computer with a USB cable. Confirm this requirement in the quotation and test it on the approved sample.

How long does the battery last?

Battery life depends on screen size, brightness, volume and video length. The battery is rechargeable by USB, and the configuration is matched to the screen size in the quotation.

What screen sizes are available?

Common screen sizes are 2.4, 4.3, 5, 7 and 10.1 inches. Compact screens suit cards and invitations, while 7 and 10.1 inch screens suit presentations.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Standard video brochure orders at Video Brochure Plus start from 10 pieces. Other formats such as boxes, cards and folders have their own minimums.
