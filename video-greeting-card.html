Three decisions to make before artwork

Keep the video focused

A compact format works best when the opening message is clear and the video is easy to follow on a smaller screen.

Separate video and printed jobs

Use video for emotion or demonstration and reserve printed space for names, dates, URLs and response instructions.

Confirm personalization early

Names, event details or multiple artwork versions affect file preparation and should be declared before pricing.

Prepare for quotation: quantity, delivery country, target date, screen preference, finished size, video duration, artwork status and packaging requirements.
