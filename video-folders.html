Three decisions to make before artwork

Choose the document format first

The paper size and number of inserts help determine whether A4 or A5 is the better starting point.

Plan screen orientation with content

Landscape video, button location and printed pockets must work together without crowding the layout.

Test repeated handling

Review hinge movement, pocket capacity, screen playback and charging access during sample approval.

Prepare for quotation: quantity, delivery country, target date, screen preference, finished size, video duration, artwork status and packaging requirements.
